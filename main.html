What is Tetris?

A simple idea that became a worldwide phenomenon.

🧩 The Game

Tetris is a puzzle game based around falling geometric pieces called Tetriminos. Players rotate and move the pieces to create complete horizontal lines.


When a line is completed, it disappears and the player receives points. As the game continues, the pieces fall faster.

👨‍💻 The Creator

Tetris was created by Soviet computer programmer Alexey Pajitnov in 1984.


Pajitnov was inspired by the puzzle game Pentominos. He created the first version of Tetris while working at the Soviet Academy of Sciences in Moscow.

A History of Tetris

The journey from Moscow to the entire world.

1984

Tetris is born

Alexey Pajitnov creates the first version of Tetris in Moscow on an Electronika 60 computer.

1985

The game spreads

Tetris is ported to the IBM PC and begins spreading throughout the Soviet Union.

1987

North America & Europe

Tetris launches on PCs in North America and Europe, helping the game expand beyond the Soviet Union.

1988

Henk Rogers discovers Tetris

Game designer and publisher Henk Rogers discovers Tetris at a trade show in Las Vegas.

1989

Game Boy changes everything

Nintendo launches the Game Boy with Tetris as a pack-in game in many markets. The portable version becomes enormously popular.

1993

Tetris goes to space

Tetris became the first video game played in space, according to Guinness World Records.

1996

The Tetris Company

Alexey Pajitnov and Henk Rogers form the business relationship that eventually becomes a key part of The Tetris Company.

2014

30 years of Tetris

Tetris celebrates its 30th anniversary and continues to appear on new platforms around the world.

2024

40 years of Tetris

Tetris celebrates four decades since its creation in 1984.
