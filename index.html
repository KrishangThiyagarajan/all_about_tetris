What is Tetris?

A simple puzzle game that became a worldwide phenomenon.

🧩 The Game

Tetris is a puzzle game where players arrange falling geometric pieces called Tetriminos.

The goal is to create complete horizontal lines. Completed lines disappear, giving the player points and more room to play.

👨‍💻 The Creator

Tetris was created by Soviet computer programmer Alexey Pajitnov in 1984.

Pajitnov created the original version while working at the Soviet Academy of Sciences in Moscow.

History of Tetris

From a Soviet computer to consoles, handhelds and modern devices.

1984

Tetris is created

Alexey Pajitnov creates the first version of Tetris in Moscow on an Electronika 60 computer.

1985

The game spreads

Tetris is ported to other computers and begins spreading outside its original environment.

1987

Tetris reaches the West

Versions of Tetris become available in North America and Europe.

1989

Game Boy

Nintendo releases the Game Boy with Tetris, helping make the game a worldwide hit.

1993

Tetris in space

Tetris becomes the first video game played in space, according to Guinness World Records.

1996

The Tetris Company

Alexey Pajitnov and Henk Rogers establish the company that manages the Tetris brand.

2014

30th Anniversary

Tetris celebrates 30 years since its creation.

2024

40th Anniversary

Tetris celebrates 40 years of entertaining players around the world.
